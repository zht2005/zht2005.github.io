I am **Hantian Zhang**, an undergraduate student in Information and Computing Science at **Jinan University**, expecting to graduate in June 2027.

My research interests lie in trustworthy machine learning, generative models, and AI security and privacy.

I am currently a visiting student at the **AGI Lab, Westlake University**, working with [Prof. Chi Zhang](https://icoz69.github.io/) and [Chenru Wang](https://winterccc.github.io/). I also work with [Prof. Qingkai Zeng](https://qingkaizeng.github.io/) at **Nankai University** and Prof. Wentai Wu at **Jinan University**.

- **Do LLMs Know When Evidence is Insufficient?** **Hantian Zhang** and Wentai Wu. *Computers, Materials & Continua*, 2026. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.32604/cmc.2026.086343)
