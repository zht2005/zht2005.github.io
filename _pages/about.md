I am **Hantian Zhang**, an undergraduate student in Information and Computing Science at **Jinan University**, expecting to graduate in June 2027. My research interests lie in trustworthy machine learning, generative models, and AI security and privacy.

I am currently a visiting student at the **AGI Lab, Westlake University**, where I work with [Chi Zhang](https://icoz69.github.io/) and [Chenru Wang](https://winterccc.github.io/) on synthetic data and diffusion guidance. I also work with [Qingkai Zeng](https://qingkaizeng.github.io/) at **Nankai University** on evidence reliability and bias in RAG, and with Wentai Wu at **Jinan University** on privacy-preserving face-image retrieval and RAG evaluation.

## Research interests

- **Reliable language models:** evidence sufficiency, answer abstention, and position bias in RAG.
- **AI security and privacy:** privacy-preserving retrieval and the utility–privacy tradeoff.
- **Generative models:** synthetic data scaling and sampling-time diffusion guidance.
